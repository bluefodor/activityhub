Portland, ME

Stop Scrolling. Go Play.

Connect with people in your area who are ready to run, climb, sail, or shoot hoops — today. Forums, plans, and chats built for actually showing up—not endless feeds.

🌊⚓🌲

Built with Greater Portland in mind

Maine’s a small state with big energy for the outdoors, pickup games, and neighborhood crews. The Activity Hub is for people here who’d rather text “we still on?” than scroll another generic feed.

Whether you’re new in town or you’ve always lived on the peninsula — find your activity, see who’s active, and make a plan. Real names, real meetups, same community you run into at the trailhead or the courts.

Why people use The Activity Hub

Everything’s organized around what you want to do — not an algorithm. A few things that make it fun 👇

😴 → 🏃

Ready Today

Flip from “inactive” to your sport when you’re actually free. Your matches see who’s around now, not who posted three weeks ago.

💬

Forums per activity

Each activity has its own space to coordinate — runs, climbs, sails, soccer, and more. Fewer random DMs, more “see you Thursday.”

📅

Plans & RSVPs

Post a time and place, see who’s in, and keep it separate from the day-to-day forum banter. Great for recurring groups and one-off meetups.

📍

Nearby, not nationwide

Distances and people lists stay focused on who’s in your area — tuned for local plans, not influencer scale.

How it works

Three steps from download to “see you there.” 🙌

1 🎯

Pick your activities

Choose what you’re into — running, climbing, pickleball, whatever you actually do. Set yourself Ready Today when you want to be discoverable.

2 🗣️

Hop in the forum

Chat in your activity’s forum, see who’s around, and react without the pressure of a huge public group chat.

3 🤝

Plan & meet up

Create or join a plan with a time and spot — then show up. That’s the whole idea: less scrolling, more playing.
